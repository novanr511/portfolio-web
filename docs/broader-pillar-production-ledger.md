## Status policy

`Drafted` means the file exists and every cited source was inspected in its original form during drafting. It does **not** mean the article has passed the master prompt's quality gate, and it does not mean it is `Ready`. An article becomes `Ready` only when its key citations have been re-checked in original sources for exact support, context, methodology/date, and limitations, its examples carry honest provenance, and it scores at least 17/20 with no zero in Accuracy, Evidence integrity, Intent, or CMS readiness.

`Reviewed` means an independent pass has checked the draft against the quality gates and returned a pass decision without expanding scope.

## Verification method

Every source in `docs/further-reading-registry.json` is checked by `scripts/verify-sources.mjs`, which fetches each URL and compares the page title (or the JSON payload) against the title recorded in the registry. All registered sources currently verify. This check caught and removed twenty-plus arXiv identifiers that pointed at unrelated papers, so the registry is the single source of truth for every reading list in the publication.

`scripts/apply-further-reading.mjs` writes the reading-list sections from the registry, so a reading list cannot drift from the sources that were actually verified. `scripts/check-mdx.mjs` compiles every article with the site's MDX pipeline and reports all failures at once, which `next build` cannot do because it stops at the first broken file.
