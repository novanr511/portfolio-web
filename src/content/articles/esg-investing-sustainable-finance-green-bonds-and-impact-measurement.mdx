Profit and purpose are converging. ESG is now mainstream.

**Direct answer:** ESG (Environmental, Social, Governance) investing integrates non-financial factors into investment decisions. Instruments: ESG integration, negative/positive screening, thematic investing, impact investing, green/social/sustainability-linked bonds. Standards: SFDR, EU Taxonomy, TCFD, ISSB, SASB, GRI. Challenges: greenwashing, data quality, double materiality.

## ESG frameworks

**Environmental:** climate change (GHG, net-zero), biodiversity, water, pollution, circular economy.

**Social:** labor rights, human capital, diversity, community relations, product liability.

**Governance:** board structure, executive pay, shareholder rights, audit, ethics, corruption.

## Investment approaches

**ESG integration:** systematic inclusion of ESG factors in financial analysis. Not exclusionary.

**Negative screening:** exclude sectors (tobacco, weapons, thermal coal, controversial weapons).

**Positive screening / Best-in-class:** select ESG leaders within sectors.

**Thematic investing:** clean energy, water, gender diversity, sustainable agriculture.

**Impact investing:** intentional measurable social/environmental impact alongside financial return. IRIS+ metrics, SDG alignment.

**Sustainability-linked bonds:** coupon step-up if KPIs (e.g., GHG reduction) not met.

## Green and sustainable bonds

**Green bonds:** proceeds for green projects (energy, transport, buildings, waste). ICMA Green Bond Principles: use of proceeds, project evaluation, management, reporting.

**Social bonds:** social outcomes (affordable housing, healthcare, education).

**Sustainability bonds:** dual green + social.

**Sustainability-linked bonds (SLBs):** general purpose, coupon tied to SPTs (Sustainability Performance Targets).

**Transition bonds:** for hard-to-abate sectors transitioning.

## Regulation and disclosure

**EU SFDR:** Sustainable Finance Disclosure Regulation. Article 6/8/9 fund classification.

**EU Taxonomy:** classification of environmentally sustainable activities. Technical screening criteria.

**CSRD / ESRS:** Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive. Double materiality (impact + financial).

**TCFD:** Task Force on Climate-related Financial Disclosures. Governance, strategy, risk, metrics.

**ISSB / IFRS S1/S2:** global baseline for sustainability disclosure.

**SASB:** industry-specific materiality maps.

**GRI:** comprehensive sustainability reporting.

## Impact measurement

**Theory of Change:** inputs → activities → outputs → outcomes → impact.

**Metrics:** IRIS+, SDG indicators, custom KPIs.

**Additionality:** would impact happen without the investment?

**Attribution:** investor's share of impact.

**Verification:** third-party assurance, second-party opinions.

## Challenges

- **Greenwashing:** misleading ESG claims. Regulation tightening.
- **Data quality:** self-reported, inconsistent, backward-looking.
- **Double materiality:** financial materiality + impact materiality.
- **Transition risk:** stranded assets, policy shifts.
