Derivatives derive value from something else. They transfer, hedge, or speculate on risk.

**Direct answer:** Derivatives are contracts whose value derives from an underlying asset (stock, bond, commodity, rate, index). Options give the right (not obligation) to buy (call) or sell (put) at a strike price by expiration. Pricing models (Black-Scholes, binomial, Monte Carlo) and Greeks (Delta, Gamma, Vega, Theta, Rho) quantify risk. Hedging uses derivatives to offset portfolio exposures.

## Option fundamentals

**Call option:** right to buy at strike K by expiry T.
**Put option:** right to sell at strike K by expiry T.
**American:** exercisable any time before expiry.
**European:** exercisable only at expiry.
**Moneyness:** ITM (in-the-money), ATM, OTM.
**Intrinsic value:** max(S-K,0) for call, max(K-S,0) for put.
**Time value:** premium - intrinsic value.

## Black-Scholes-Merton model

Assumes: lognormal returns, constant volatility, no dividends, frictionless markets, risk-free rate constant.

Formula (call): C = S₀N(d₁) - Ke^(-rT)N(d₂)
d₁ = [ln(S/K) + (r + σ²/2)T] / (σ√T)
d₂ = d₁ - σ√T

**Assumptions often violated:** volatility smile/skew, fat tails, jump diffusion, stochastic volatility.

## The Greeks

| Greek | Measures | Formula (Call) | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Delta (Δ)** | ∂V/∂S | N(d₁) | Price sensitivity to underlying. Hedge ratio. |
| **Gamma (Γ)** | ∂²V/∂S² | φ(d₁)/(Sσ√T) | Convexity. Delta stability. |
| **Vega (ν)** | ∂V/∂σ | Sφ(d₁)√T | Volatility sensitivity. |
| **Theta (Θ)** | ∂V/∂T | -Sφ(d₁)σ/(2√T) - rKe^(-rT)N(d₂) | Time decay. |
| **Rho (ρ)** | ∂V/∂r | TKe^(-rT)N(d₂) | Interest rate sensitivity. |

**Portfolio Greeks:** sum of position Greeks. Delta-neutral: Σ Δᵢ × qtyᵢ = 0.

## Volatility surfaces

Implied volatility varies by strike and expiry: **vol smile** (U-shape for equities), **vol skew** (puts > calls for equities), **term structure** (vol by tenor). SABR, Heston, local vol, stochastic vol models fit surfaces.

## Hedging strategies

**Delta hedging:** dynamically rebalance to maintain Δ=0. Continuous in theory, discrete in practice → gamma/theta costs.

**Delta-gamma hedging:** add options to neutralize gamma.

**Vega hedging:** use options to offset vega exposure.

**Static hedging:** replicate exotic payoff with vanilla options (static replication).

**Delta-one products:** futures, forwards, swaps, ETFs. Delta ≈ 1.

## Exotic options

- **Barrier:** knock-in/out at barrier level.
- **Asian:** average price payoff.
- **Lookback:** payoff on min/max.
- **Binary/Digital:** fixed payout if ITM.
- **Compound:** option on option.

## Applications

- **Portfolio insurance:** protective puts, collars.
- **Covered calls:** income generation.
- **Dispersion trading:** index vs single-stock vol.
- **Volatility trading:** long/short vol, variance swaps.
