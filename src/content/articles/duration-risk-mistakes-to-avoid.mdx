## The mistakes

<Trap>
**Treating duration as an exact forecast.**
</Trap>

<Mark>The approximation is local</Mark>, which the duration entry explains, as [Convexity Explained](/articles/convexity-explained) treats the curvature that the linear measure misses, as the worked example below shows.
