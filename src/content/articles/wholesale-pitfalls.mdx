## The pitfalls

**Confusing sell-in with sell-through.** <Mark>A large order to a retailer is inventory moving to a warehouse, not necessarily to a customer</Mark>, which the retail-channel article treats as a distinction between channels, as the inventory article treats stock as a cash decision, as the demand-generation article advises on preference before capture.
