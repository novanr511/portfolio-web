Management is not about control. It is about enabling.

**Direct answer:** Engineering management builds and sustains high-performing engineering teams. Core responsibilities: hiring (sourcing, interviewing, closing), onboarding (ramp, buddy, 30/60/90), performance management (feedback, reviews, PIPs), career development (ladders, growth plans), culture (psychological safety, inclusion, accountability), and metrics (delivery, quality, health, satisfaction). Great managers amplify team output; bad managers become bottlenecks.

## Hiring

**Define the role:** scope, level, must-haves, nice-to-haves, team context. Avoid laundry lists.

**Sourcing:** referrals (highest signal), outbound (LinkedIn, GitHub, Stack Overflow), agencies (specialized), university (new grad), branding (blog, talks, open source).

**Interview process:**
- **Screen:** recruiter (logistics, baseline), hiring manager (motivation, trajectory).
- **Technical:** coding (live, not LeetCode), system design (scope, trade-offs), debugging (real bug), architecture review (past project).
- **Behavioral:** STAR (Situation, Task, Action, Result). Collaboration, conflict, failure, growth.
- **Values/culture:** alignment with principles, not "culture fit" (bias risk).

**Decision:** debrief same day. Hire/no-hire with evidence. Consensus not required; hiring manager decides with input.

**Offer:** competitive compensation (base, equity, benefits), transparency (band, percentile), deadline (1 week), celebration.

## Onboarding

**Pre-start:** equipment, accounts, access, welcome package, buddy assigned.

**Week 1:** orientation, team intros, codebase tour, dev environment, first commit (trivial fix).

**Month 1:** first feature (small, scoped), buddy check-ins daily, manager 1:1 weekly.

**Month 3:** first significant project, feedback loop (360), confirm ramp trajectory.

**Buddy system:** assigned peer, not manager. Social + technical guide.

## Performance management

**Continuous feedback:** weekly 1:1 (not status update), real-time feedback (SBI: Situation, Behavior, Impact), public praise, private correction.

**Reviews:** semi-annual. Self-reflection → peer feedback → manager assessment → calibration → discussion → documented.

**Calibration:** cross-team consistency. Avoid grade inflation/deflation.

**PIP (Performance Improvement Plan):** last resort. Specific, measurable, time-bound (30–90 days), support provided, documented. Outcome: improve, transition, or exit.

## Career ladders

**Dual track:** IC (Individual Contributor) and Manager tracks. Equivalent levels, different scope.

**Levels (example):**
- **L1/L2:** Junior / Mid. Execute tasks, learn.
- **L3:** Senior. Own features, mentor, design.
- **L4:** Staff. Cross-team, architecture, strategy.
- **L5:** Principal. Org-wide, technical vision, industry influence.
- **L6:** Distinguished. Company-wide, industry-defining.

**Manager track:** EM (line), Senior EM (managers), Director (multi-team), VP (org).

**Competencies per level:** technical depth, scope, influence, communication, judgment, people (for managers).

**Promotion:** evidence-based (scope, impact, consistency). Packet: self-nom + peer/manager support. Committee reviews.

## Culture building

**Psychological safety:** speak up, admit mistakes, ask questions, challenge ideas. Google Project Aristotle: #1 predictor of team effectiveness.

**Inclusion:** diverse hiring, equitable processes, belonging (ERGs, mentorship, sponsorship).

**Accountability:** clear expectations, follow-through, blameless postmortems.

**Learning culture:** blameless postmortems, learning budget, tech talks, hack weeks, conference budget.

**Recognition:** public praise, peer bonuses, spot awards, promotion celebration.

**Decision-making:** RACI, decision log, disagree and commit.

## Metrics that matter

**Delivery:** lead time (commit → deploy), deploy frequency, change failure rate, MTTR (DORA four keys).

**Quality:** defect escape rate, test coverage (meaningful), static analysis findings, security vulnerabilities.

**Health:** on-call burden (pages/week), alert fatigue, tech debt index, architecture fitness.

**People:** eNPS, retention (regrettable vs non-regrettable), promotion rate, internal mobility, 1:1 quality.

**Business:** feature cycle time, customer satisfaction (NPS/CSAT), revenue per engineer.

**Manager self-assessment:** 1:1 quality, team health, hiring success, retention, delivery predictability.

## Common pitfalls

- **Hero mode:** manager codes, becomes bottleneck.
- **Conflict avoidance:** delays feedback, erodes trust.
- **Micromanagement:** prescribes how, not what.
- **Neglecting high performers:** they leave silently.
- **Hiring clones:** homogeneity kills innovation.
- **Ignoring tech debt:** compounds into paralysis.
