Crises don't build character. They reveal it.

**Direct answer:** Crisis management prepares for, responds to, and recovers from events that threaten reputation, operations, or stakeholder trust. A crisis plan defines roles, escalation paths, communication protocols, and decision authority. Speed, transparency, empathy, and accountability determine whether a crisis becomes a catastrophe or a credibility builder.

## Crisis types

**Product:** safety recall, quality defect, security vulnerability, data breach.
**Operational:** outage, supply chain disruption, facility incident.
**Reputational:** executive misconduct, social media firestorm, whistleblower, activist campaign.
**Financial:** fraud, restatement, bankruptcy, liquidity crisis.
**Regulatory:** investigation, enforcement action, fine, sanction.
**Natural/force majeure:** pandemic, natural disaster, geopolitical event.

## Crisis preparation

**Crisis management team (CMT):** CEO (decision), CCO/CMO (comms), CLO (legal), COO (ops), CISO (security), CFO (finance), CHRO (people). Defined roles, backups, 24/7 contact.

**Crisis playbook:** scenarios, decision trees, holding statements, escalation matrix, stakeholder maps, approved spokespeople, media lists, monitoring keywords.

**Monitoring:** 24/7 social listening (Brandwatch, Talkwalker, Meltwater), news alerts, employee channels, customer support spikes, analyst/investor chatter.

**Simulation:** tabletop exercises (quarterly), red team exercises, media training for spokespeople.

## Response framework (first 24 hours)

**1. Acknowledge (0–1 hr):** "We are aware. We are investigating. We will update at [time]." No speculation, no blame, no minimization.

**2. Assess (1–4 hr):** facts confirmed, impact scope, stakeholders affected, legal/regulatory obligations.

**3. Activate (4–12 hr):** CMT convened, holding statement → detailed statement, stakeholder notifications (customers, employees, regulators, investors, partners, media).

**4. Communicate (ongoing):** regular cadence (hourly → daily), single source of truth (dark site / microsite), FAQ updated, media briefings, employee town hall.

## Communication principles

- **Speed > perfection:** early partial info > late perfect info.
- **Transparency:** what you know, what you don't, what you're doing.
- **Empathy:** human impact first, then facts.
- **Accountability:** "We own this. Here's what we're fixing."
- **Consistency:** one voice, one message, all channels.
- **No speculation:** "We don't know yet, we'll update at X."

## Stakeholder-specific communication

- **Customers:** direct email, in-app banner, support script, refund/replacement process.
- **Employees:** internal memo, town hall, manager talking points, EAP resources.
- **Regulators:** formal notification, cooperation, timeline, remediation plan.
- **Investors/Board:** 8-K/Form 8-K, board call, recovery plan, financial impact.
- **Media:** press release, media briefing, designated spokesperson, media kit.
- **Partners/Customers:** direct outreach, joint communication plan.

## Post-crisis recovery

**Root cause analysis (RCA):** 5 Whys, fishbone, timeline reconstruction. Blameless culture.

**Remediation plan:** fixes, timeline, owners, verification, communication.

**Trust rebuilding:** transparency reports, third-party audit, customer advisory board, enhanced safeguards.

**Reputation monitoring:** sentiment tracking, share of voice, brand health tracking (quarterly).

**Lessons learned:** post-mortem (blameless), playbook updates, training updates, tabletop schedule.

## Legal and regulatory

- **Privilege:** legal holds, attorney-client privilege, work product doctrine.
- **Notifications:** breach notification laws (GDPR 72h, state laws, sector-specific).
- **Preservation:** litigation hold, evidence preservation, chain of custody.
- **Insurance:** cyber insurance, D&O, E&O, business interruption. Notify carriers early.
