Acquisition gets customers. Success keeps and grows them.

**Direct answer:** Customer Success (CS) is the proactive function ensuring customers achieve desired outcomes with your product, driving retention, expansion, and advocacy. In recurring revenue models, CS owns the post-sale lifecycle: onboarding → adoption → renewal → expansion. Net Revenue Retention (NRR) > 100% is the north star metric.

## The CS lifecycle

1. **Onboarding (0–30/90 days):** kickoff, configuration, training, first value milestone. Goal: Time-to-Value (TTV) minimized.
2. **Adoption (ongoing):** feature adoption, usage depth, stakeholder engagement. Goal: habit formation.
3. **Renewal (90–180 days pre-renewal):** health check, business review, negotiation, contract execution.
4. **Expansion (continuous):** upsell (higher tier), cross-sell (adjacent modules), seat expansion, usage-based growth.

## Onboarding excellence

- **Kickoff:** align on success criteria, stakeholders, timeline.
- **Technical setup:** SSO, integrations, data migration, sandbox.
- **Training:** role-based (admin, end-user, champion), live + async.
- **Milestones:** first login → first core action → first outcome → referenceable.
- **CSM assignment:** segment by ARR, complexity, strategic value.

## Health scoring

**Health score = weighted composite of:**
- **Usage:** logins, core actions, feature adoption depth, session duration.
- **Engagement:** NPS/CSAT, support tickets (volume/sentiment), community participation.
- **Outcome:** ROI metrics achieved, goal completion, business impact documented.
- **Relationship:** stakeholder access, executive sponsor engagement, champion strength.

**Scoring model:** 0–100 or Red/Yellow/Green. Weighted by predictive power (logistic regression on churn/expansion).

**Action triggers:**
- **Red:** executive escalation, rescue plan.
- **Yellow:** proactive outreach, targeted training.
- **Green:** expansion conversation, advocacy ask.

## Renewal management

**Timeline:** 180 days out → forecast → 90 days → business review → 30 days → contract → renewal.

**Renewal playbook:**
- **Risk assessment:** health score, stakeholder changes, competitive threat, budget cycles.
- **Business review (QBR/EBR):** outcomes delivered, roadmap alignment, expansion opportunities.
- **Negotiation:** anchor on value delivered, not discount. Multi-year for discount.
- **Churn risk mitigation:** executive sponsor engagement, executive sponsor, executive sponsor.

## Expansion playbooks

**Upsell (higher tier):** feature gating, usage limits, advanced analytics, premium support.

**Cross-sell (adjacent modules):** "customers like you also bought," solution bundles.

**Seat expansion:** team growth, new departments, partner access.

**Usage-based:** overage triggers, tier upgrades, enterprise agreements.

**Expansion motions:**
- **CSM-owned:** relationship-based, high-touch.
- **Product-led (PQL):** in-product prompts, self-serve upgrade.
- **Sales-assisted:** CSM qualifies, AE closes.

## Metrics that matter

- **GRR (Gross Revenue Retention):** (Starting MRR - Churn - Contraction) / Starting MRR. Floor.
- **NRR (Net Revenue Retention):** (Starting MRR + Expansion - Churn - Contraction) / Starting MRR. Growth engine.
- **Logo retention:** % customers renewed.
- **Time-to-value (TTV):** onboarding speed.
- **Expansion rate:** % ARR from expansion.
- **CSM capacity:** accounts per CSM, ARR per CSM.

## Scaling CS

- **Segmentation:** High-touch (CSM), Tech-touch (automated), Low-touch (self-serve).
- **Digital CS:** in-app guides, email nurture, webinars, community, AI chatbots.
- **CS Ops:** tooling (Gainsight, Totango, ChurnZero, Vitally), health score automation, playbook library, QBR templates.
