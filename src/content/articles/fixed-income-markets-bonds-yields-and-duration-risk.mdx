Bonds are loans. Understanding their math is essential for portfolio construction.

**Direct answer:** Fixed income markets trade debt securities that promise fixed or floating cash flows. Key concepts: price-yield inverse relationship, duration (interest rate sensitivity), convexity, credit spreads, and yield curve dynamics. Bonds provide income, capital preservation, and diversification against equity risk.

## Bond mechanics

**Price-yield relationship:** price and yield move inversely. When yields rise, prices fall.

**Coupon:** periodic interest payment (fixed or floating).

**Yield to Maturity (YTM):** internal rate of return if held to maturity, assuming reinvestment at YTM.

**Current yield:** annual coupon / current price.

**Clean vs dirty price:** clean excludes accrued interest; dirty = clean + accrued.

## Duration and convexity

**Macaulay duration:** weighted average time to receive cash flows (years).

**Modified duration:** percentage price change for 1% yield change. %ΔP ≈ -ModDur × Δy.

**Convexity:** curvature of price-yield relationship. Positive convexity = price rises more than duration predicts when yields fall, falls less when yields rise.

**Key insight:** long-duration bonds have higher interest rate risk. Portfolio managers match duration to liabilities (immunization).

## Yield curve

**Term structure:** yields across maturities. Shapes: normal (upward), inverted, flat, humped.

**Theories:** expectations hypothesis, liquidity preference, market segmentation.

**Inversion:** short rates > long rates. Historically predicts recession (lead time 6-24 months).

**Key rates:** 2s10s spread, 3m10y spread, Fed funds rate.

## Credit risk and spreads

**Investment grade (IG):** BBB- and above (S&P/Fitch), Baa3+ (Moody's). Lower default risk.

**High yield (HY) / Junk:** below IG. Higher yield compensates default risk.

**Spreads:** credit spread = corporate yield - risk-free (Treasury) yield. Wider = more risk.

**CDS (Credit Default Swaps):** insurance against default. Spread = annual premium.

**Ratings:** AAA → D. Watch for fallen angels (IG→HY) and rising stars (HY→IG).

## Bond types

- **Government:** Treasuries (risk-free benchmark), TIPS (inflation-linked), Agency (GSE).
- **Municipal:** tax-exempt interest. GO (full faith) vs Revenue (project-backed).
- **Corporate:** IG, HY, convertible, callable, putable.
- **Securitized:** MBS, ABS, CMBS, CLO. Prepayment risk, tranching.
- **International:** sovereign, emerging market (EM), currency risk.

## Portfolio construction

- **Barbell:** short + long maturities, avoid middle.
- **Bullet:** concentrate at target duration.
- **Ladder:** equal weights across maturities.
- **Core-plus:** core IG + satellite HY/EM.
