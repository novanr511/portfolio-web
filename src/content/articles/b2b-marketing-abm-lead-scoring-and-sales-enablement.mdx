B2B marketing sells to committees, not individuals.

**Direct answer:** B2B marketing targets organizations through multi-threaded engagement. Core strategies: Account-Based Marketing (ABM) for high-value targets, lead scoring to prioritize pipeline, sales enablement to convert opportunities. Alignment between marketing, sales, and customer success around shared revenue metrics (pipeline, bookings, NRR) is the differentiator.

## Account-Based Marketing (ABM)

**ABM tiers:**
- **1:1 (Strategic):** top 10-50 accounts. Bespoke campaigns, executive engagement, custom content.
- **1:Few (Lite):** 50-100 accounts. Semi-personalized programs, industry verticals.
- **1:Many (Programmatic):** 100-1000+ accounts. Tech-enabled personalization, intent data.

**ABM workflow:**
1. **Select accounts:** ICP fit, intent signals, revenue potential.
2. **Insights:** account research, stakeholder map, tech stack, initiatives.
3. **Engage:** multi-channel (ads, email, direct mail, events, social, direct outreach).
4. **Measure:** engagement score, pipeline influenced, revenue attributed.

**Tech stack:** 6sense, Demandbase, Terminus, RollWorks, HubSpot ABM, LinkedIn Matched Audiences.

## Lead scoring

**Explicit (fit):** firmographics (industry, size, revenue, tech stack), demographics (title, seniority, department).

**Implicit (intent):** website behavior (pricing page, docs, demo request), content consumption, email engagement, event attendance, third-party intent (Bombora, G2, TechTarget).

**Scoring model:**
- **Explicit + Implicit = Fit + Intent.**
- **Thresholds:** MQL threshold, SQL threshold, PQL threshold (if PLG).
- **Decay:** score decays with inactivity (half-life).

**Calibration:** regular review with sales. Track conversion rates by score band. Adjust weights quarterly.

## Sales enablement

**Content:** battle cards, competitor one-pagers, case studies, ROI calculators, proposal templates, objection handling guides.

**Training:** onboarding, continuous (call coaching, role-play, competitive intelligence), certification.

**Tools:** CRM (SFDC, HubSpot), conversation intelligence (Gong, Chorus), content management (Highspot, Seismic), proposal (PandaDoc), scheduling (Calendly).

**Process:** lead routing (round-robin, territory, account-based), SLA (MQL→SQL time), qualification framework (MEDDPICC, BANT, SPICED).

## Demand generation engine

**Top of funnel:** content (SEO, webinars, reports), paid (LinkedIn, search, review sites), events (owned, sponsored), PR/analyst relations.

**Middle of funnel:** nurture sequences, retargeting, personalized outreach, trial/freemium onboarding.

**Bottom of funnel:** case studies, references, security/compliance docs, procurement support, contract negotiation.

## Metrics that matter

- **Pipeline generated:** marketing-sourced + marketing-influenced.
- **MQL → SQL conversion rate.**
- **Sales cycle length.**
- **Win rate by segment/channel.**
- **CAC payback period.**
- **LTV:CAC by segment.**
- **Marketing % of revenue.**

**Alignment:** shared OKRs (pipeline, revenue), weekly pipeline review, joint QBRs.
