A small team buys insurance to handle a loss it could not absorb, which is the basic mechanism of risk transfer. The mistake is treating a policy as the whole risk plan: deductibles, exclusions, and limits decide what is actually covered, and the team still owns prevention and records, as the risk-assessment article treats residual risk as something to state, as the incident-response article treats containment as a designed process.

**Direct answer:** Buy insurance for risks that are severe and hard to self-insure, read the exclusions and limits, and keep the controls that reduce the chance of a claim.
