Reddit mentions are the closest single tracker of AI visibility in the largest published comparison, and the number carrying that claim is a rank correlation. The decision a team can make from it is where to look first, not how much visibility a campaign will buy.

## Which mention types track AI visibility most closely?

Ahrefs studied seven mention types across 10,000 US brands and 260 million non-branded prompts on 3 November 2026. Reddit threads rank first at 0.75 Spearman, ahead of domains and webpages at 0.70, news at 0.67, YouTube at 0.64, and TikTok at 0.60. The number orders brands; it is a correlation, not evidence that a mention caused visibility.

Spearman's rho asks whether two orderings line up. Here it compares how often a brand is named in AI answers with how often it is named on each mention surface. A value near 1 means brands keep roughly the same positions on both lists. The seven values sit between 0.60 and 0.75; the narrow span is why the source concludes that none should be discarded.

The source offers a mechanism for Reddit's lead: Reddit licenses content to Google and OpenAI, giving those systems direct access to its posts. Ahrefs also reports it as the second most cited domain in its prompt database, behind Wikipedia. That is the publisher's interpretation of an observed ranking. The same correlation fits a well-known brand attracting both kinds of attention, and the coefficient cannot separate the stories.

Notice what was counted. "Reddit mentions" means threads that rank in Google and name the brand, not every Reddit post ever written. YouTube counts transcripts, TikTok counts descriptions and transcripts, and off-site web counts exclude the brand's own website. These are defined measures, and the ranking is only as good as those definitions.

## Does a mention on a high-traffic page count for more?

Ahrefs reported 0.70 for raw webpage mentions and the same 0.70 when those mentions were weighted by the organic traffic of the pages carrying them. Page traffic adds nothing to the association with AI brand mentions. Breadth across sites is the pattern in this comparison; the authority of a single host did not separate brands.

That result is inconvenient for the usual pitching logic, where one large placement is treated as worth many small ones. In this dataset, a mention on a quiet page tracks AI visibility about as closely as one on a busy page. The source reads "mentions are the new links" as being talked about widely rather than by a few loud sites.

Care is needed with "worth." The study measured association, not placement value. Two brands with identical mention counts can differ in how answers describe them, and the coefficient says nothing about that. The equal 0.70 figures support a decision about where to allocate attention: a wider set of sites is at least as defensible as one big name. Traffic was measured as a correlate, and as a correlate it added nothing.

## How much does AI visibility grow when mentions grow?

Ahrefs found diminishing returns: a brand with ten times the webpage mentions typically showed about 3.7 times the AI mentions, roughly 73 versus 273 in their example. Ten times the Reddit mentions tracked about 5.5 times the AI mentions. Those multipliers are source-reported correlations from 3 November 2026, so they describe a gradient, not the yield of a campaign.

Moving from 1,000 to 10,000 webpage mentions adds 9,000 pages and about 200 AI mentions in the reported example. A handful of placements sits far below the scale where the pattern becomes visible. The same sublinear shape appears on every platform, strongest on AI Overviews at about 3.5 times and weakest on ChatGPT at about 2.2.

Reddit is steeper: ten times the mentions tracks about 5.5 times the AI mentions. The typical brand appeared in eight Reddit threads, and 29% in none. A team below that median has a visible gap; one above it should expect diminishing returns to reappear.

These multipliers describe brands ordered by an existing count. They are not an elasticity and cannot be applied to a planned spend. Calling them a forecast turns a correlation into a causal claim the study explicitly declines to make. The honest use is to set an expectation of scale and duration: a visible change, if mentions are involved at all, would need a sustained increase observed over months.

## Is the pattern just a side effect of brand size?

Ahrefs split the same 10,000 brands into monthly search-volume bands. Within each band, webpage mentions still correlated with AI mentions at 0.34 to 0.54, versus 0.70 across the full sample. Brand size explains part of the pattern, since the within-band values are lower, but the ordering survives in every band. Correlation still cannot settle which direction the influence runs.

The published bands put numbers on that qualification: under 500 monthly brand searches, 2,452 brands at 0.545; 500 to 2,000, 2,419 brands at 0.34; 2,000 to 10,000, 2,595 brands at 0.36; 10,000 to 50,000, 1,632 brands at 0.40; and above 50,000, 902 brands at 0.46. The source also reports that the pattern held across 50 finer groups of near-identical demand.

Holding search volume roughly constant weakens the correlation, which is the expected result if big brands attract both more mentions and more AI attention. But a value above zero inside every band means size alone does not account for the ordering. That is a genuine narrowing of the explanation, and it is still an observational narrowing. Well-known brands may differ in ways the bands do not capture, and mentions may be a marker of that difference rather than its driver.

For a decision, this is the most useful caveat in the study. It says the signal is not purely an artefact of brand size, so it is worth testing on a mid-sized brand. It does not say the signal is a mechanism.

## What should a team decide with a coefficient of 0.75?

Treat the ranking as a prior for where to test, not a forecast of return. Pick the surface with the closest association, record a baseline of unbranded mentions and AI appearances, change one input, and read the result over a fixed window of months. Keep a holdout. A correlation can choose a lever; only a controlled change can price it.

Fix the prompt set before the change, exclude prompts containing your own brand name to measure discovery rather than recall, and count mentions and citations separately. A rise in either is still an observation; the holdout is what makes it interpretable. Without one, both can share a cause such as a product launch, and the attributive story looks convincing while remaining untested.

A practical warning comes from a different measurement job. The [RAG research agent build log](/projects/rag-research-agent) describes a 193-check consistency audit comparing numbers in a dashboard and reports with the pipeline files. On 18 November 2026 it reported 0 failed checks, alongside 55 passed tests and 1 skipped. The build log states the boundary plainly: agreement with artifacts is validity of the write-up, not validity of the method. A 0.75 coefficient deserves the same treatment. It is coherent evidence about ordering, and a separate question remains about whether changing mentions changes answers.

## How is this different from a customer advocacy programme?

Advocacy is an intervention you run with customers; these coefficients describe third-party conversations you observe. A reward programme changes who speaks, while this study counts where brands were already discussed. The Ahrefs comparison cannot tell you whether asking customers helped. Its use here is narrower: it ranks observational signals worth testing, and it leaves program design to a different question.

The two are easy to merge because both produce a count of people saying things. An advocacy programme asks for a recommendation and often pays for it, which changes the population being measured: people motivated by the reward speak, and quiet customers stay quiet. The mention study observes conversations that happened without its involvement, which is a different evidence base with different biases. It also counts off-site surfaces, not customer relationships.

That distinction is why these results should sit beside [Customer advocacy and user-generated content](/articles/customer-advocacy-and-user-generated-content) rather than inside it. The advocacy piece is about what a programme can and cannot claim. This piece is about what can be inferred from a correlation between existing mentions and AI answers. The answer here is: a hypothesis about where to look, with no instruction to launch a programme and no estimate of its return.

## What do these correlations fail to establish?

They do not establish causation, traffic, clicks, or revenue. The study covers 10,000 US brands, non-branded prompts, and three answer surfaces in 2026; it ran no intervention. ChatGPT was weakest across six of seven factors, and 46% of brands were invisible there alone. The coefficient describes where a brand tends to appear, not what a page change would buy.

Across all three surfaces, 14% of brands received zero AI mentions, versus 46% in ChatGPT, 23% in AI Mode, and 18% in AI Overviews. Brands at zero are hard to order, which affects the coefficient. The pooled figure is higher than any single-platform value partly because fewer brands sit at zero when the three are combined. That is a property of the measurement, not a better outcome.

The study also says nothing about clicks. A brand can appear more often in answers and receive fewer visits if the answer satisfies the question. For the difference between appearance and influence, see [Zero-click content metrics that matter](/articles/zero-click-content-metrics-that-matter).

The decision I would make is modest. Rank the surfaces by correlation, test Reddit and broad web coverage first on a fixed prompt set with a holdout, and report the result as a change in appearances with its uncertainty. I would not multiply a coefficient into a traffic forecast. Correlation is where the investigation starts, and repeating that matters more here than any single number in the table.
