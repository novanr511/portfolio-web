## The approach

**Fix the component, not the page.** A defect in a shared button appears everywhere the button is used, which the design-system article treats as the unit of change, as the component-library article advises for governance, as [Technical Debt: The Hidden Cost of Shortcuts](/articles/technical-debt-the-hidden-cost-of-shortcuts) warns about repeated fixes.

**Automate what can be checked.** Missing labels, contrast, and structure can be checked in CI, which the testing article advises for a gate, as the evaluation-rubric article requires for concrete criteria, as the linting article treats mechanical checks as a baseline.

**Test journeys with assistive technology.** Keyboard and screen-reader paths must work in practice, which MDN's accessibility reference covers, as the usability-testing method advises for real users, as the customer-journey article argues for evidence from real people.

**Keep a record.** Which components are affected, which checks run, and which journeys were tested, which the data-governance article requires for provenance, as the decision-memo article requires for a recorded judgement.
