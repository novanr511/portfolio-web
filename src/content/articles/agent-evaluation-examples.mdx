## Further Reading

- Percy Liang et al., 2022. [Holistic Evaluation of Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.09110) — HELM's multi-metric framework: 7 metrics across 16 core scenarios, 30 models, and the finding that models were previously evaluated on only 17.9% of shared scenarios.
- Joon Sung Park et al., 2023. [Generative Agents: Interactive Simulacra of Human Behavior](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.03442) — An architecture for agent memory: observations, reflection and planning, with retrieval of stored experiences.
