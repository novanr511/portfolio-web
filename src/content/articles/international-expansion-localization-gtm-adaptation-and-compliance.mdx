Global is not "launch everywhere." It is a sequence of deliberate market entries.

**Direct answer:** International expansion requires adapting product, go-to-market, operations, and compliance for each target market. Success factors: market prioritization (TAM, readiness, competitive intensity), localization depth (language, currency, legal, cultural), GTM adaptation (channel, pricing, positioning), entity setup, tax/compliance, and local team hiring. The playbook: prioritize → validate → launch → scale.

## Market prioritization framework

**Score each market on:**
- **TAM/SAM:** addressable revenue, growth rate.
- **Product-market fit readiness:** local demand signals (traffic, searches, competitors), willingness to pay.
- **Competitive intensity:** incumbents, switching costs, differentiation defensibility.
- **Regulatory complexity:** data residency, licensing, certifications, sector-specific rules.
- **Operational complexity:** time zones, language, talent pool, payment rails.
- **Strategic value:** beachhead for region, reference customers, talent hub.

**Score → Tier 1 (launch now), Tier 2 (12–18 months), Tier 3 (watch).**

## Localization depth

**Language:** translation (UI, docs, support, legal), locale (date, number, currency formats), RTL support, font fallbacks.

**Cultural:** color symbolism, imagery, humor, holidays, social norms, payment preferences (cash, wallet, BNPL, bank transfer).

**Legal/regulatory:** entity type (subsidiary, branch, PEO, EOR), data residency (GDPR, LGPD, PDPA, PIPL), licensing, certifications, consumer protection, advertising rules.

**Technical:** CDN/CDN edge, data residency (region-locked DB), latency, CDN, local payment gateways, SMS/email providers.

**Support:** local language support hours, local phone numbers, local SLA.

## GTM adaptation

**Positioning:** reframe value prop for local pain points (e.g., "GDPR-compliant" in EU, "data sovereignty" in China).

**Pricing:** local currency, purchasing power parity (PPP) adjustment, local payment methods, tax-inclusive vs exclusive.

**Channels:** direct (inside sales, field), channel (resellers, SIs, distributors), marketplace (cloud marketplaces, local app stores), partnerships (local SIs, telcos, banks).

**Marketing:** local SEO/SEM (Baidu, Yandex, Naver), local social (WeChat, LINE, KakaoTalk), local events, local PR/analyst relations.

**Sales motion:** inside vs field, hunter/farmer split, local quota, local comp plan.

## Entity and compliance

**Entity models:**
- **EOR (Employer of Record):** hire without entity. Fast, limited control.
- **PEO (Professional Employer Org):** co-employment. HR/admin outsourced.
- **Branch:** extension of parent. Simple, parent liability.
- **Subsidiary:** separate legal entity. Full control, full compliance.

**Tax:** permanent establishment (PE) risk, transfer pricing, VAT/GST, withholding tax, double tax treaties.

**Data:** GDPR (EU), LGPD (Brazil), PDPA (Singapore/Thailand), PIPL (China), CCPA/CPRA (US), APPI (Japan). DPIA, DPA, SCCs, adequacy decisions.

**Sector-specific:** fintech (licensing), health (HIPAA, MDR), telecom (licensing), crypto (MiCA, VARA).

## Operational readiness

**Team:** first hire = Country Manager / GM. Then: sales, marketing, CS, support, legal/finance, HR.

**Payroll/benefits:** local payroll provider, statutory benefits, equity (ESOP/RSU) localization.

**Banking:** local bank account, FX hedging, repatriation.

**IP protection:** trademark registration (Madrid Protocol), patent filing (PCT), copyright, trade secrets.

**Security:** ISO 27001, SOC 2, local certifications (IRAP, CSML, ISO 27001 local).

## Launch playbook

1. **Research (M-6 to M-3):** market study, legal audit, competitor map, partner shortlist.
2. **Validate (M-3 to M-1):** beta customers, pilot partners, legal entity, banking.
3. **Launch (M0):** localized product, localized GTM, local team, compliance live.
4. **Scale (M+1 to M+12):** hire, iterate, partner ecosystem, local events, PR.
