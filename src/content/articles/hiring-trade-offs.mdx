## The trade-offs

**Capacity versus clarity.** A hire adds hands, not definition, which the O*NET-style task descriptions help with, as the value-proposition article treats clarity as prior, as the decision-memo article requires a specific question. An undefined role produces the same confusion with more people, as the team-culture article warns about implicit norms.

**Speed versus fit.** Hiring quickly to relieve pressure can lower the bar, which the hiring-checklist article treats as a set of deliberate steps, as the behavioral-interview article advises for evidence, as the evaluation-rubric article requires for concrete criteria.

**Generalist versus specialist.** A specialist solves a narrow problem well; a generalist covers more ground, which the T-shaped-professional article treats as a combination, as the O*NET-style task descriptions help define the need, as the engineering-management article advises on career ladders.
