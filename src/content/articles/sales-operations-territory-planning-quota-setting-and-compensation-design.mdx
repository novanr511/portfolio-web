Sales ops is the engine room of revenue. Strategy without ops is hallucination.

**Direct answer:** Sales Operations (Sales Ops) designs and operates the infrastructure that enables sales teams to sell efficiently: territory design, quota setting, compensation plans, forecasting, CRM hygiene, and analytics. Great ops turns sales from art into a repeatable, scalable engine.

## Territory planning

**Design principles:**
- **Equity:** equal opportunity (TAM, account count, propensity).
- **Compactness:** geographic contiguity (field) or logical grouping (vertical, size).
- **Balance:** equal quota potential, workload.
- **Continuity:** minimize disruption year-over-year.

**Inputs:** account scoring (firmographics, intent, propensity), historical revenue, rep capacity, travel time.

**Models:**
- **Geographic:** zip codes, DMAs, states.
- **Vertical/Industry:** SaaS, FinTech, Healthcare, Manufacturing.
- **Named accounts:** strategic top-100 assigned individually.
- **Hybrid:** named + geographic remainder.

**Tools:** territory mapping (Fullcast, Varicent, Xactly, Salesforce Maps), capacity models.

## Quota setting

**Methodologies:**
- **Bottom-up:** rep capacity × win rate × avg deal size × sales cycle.
- **Top-down:** revenue target / headcount + buffer.
- **Historical:** prior year attainment + growth factor.
- **Market-based:** TAM × share target / reps.

**Best practices:**
- **Bottom-up + top-down reconciliation:** quotas sum to corporate target + 10–15% buffer.
- **Attainability:** 60–70% of reps should hit quota (not 100%).
- **Ramp quotas:** new hires at 50%/75%/100% over ramp period.
- **Mid-year adjustments:** only for structural changes (territory, product, M&A).

**Quota types:** revenue (ARR/MRR), bookings, units, activities (demos, proposals).

## Compensation design

**Plan components:**
- **Base salary:** 50–60% of OTE (On-Target Earnings).
- **Variable (commission):** 40–50% of OTE.
- **Accelerators:** >100% attainment → higher rate (e.g., 1.5x, 2x).
- **Decelerators:** <50% attainment → reduced rate.
- **SPIFFs:** short-term incentives for strategic pushes.

**Plan types:**
- **Hunter (new logo):** higher variable, aggressive accelerators.
- **Farmer (expansion/renewal):** lower variable, retention/expansion metrics.
- **Hybrid:** blend.

**Key design principles:**
- **Simplicity:** rep can calculate their check mentally.
- **Alignment:** comp drives desired behavior (new logo vs expansion vs retention).
- **Timeliness:** monthly/quarterly payouts, clear statements.
- **Clawbacks:** for churn within 90–180 days.

## Forecasting accuracy

**Forecast categories:**
- **Commit:** high confidence (>90%).
- **Best case:** commit + upside (60–90%).
- **Pipeline:** all qualified opportunities (20–60%).

**Forecasting methods:**
- **Rep commit:** bottom-up, subjective.
- **Stage-weighted:** Σ (stage probability × amount).
- **Time-series / ML:** historical patterns, seasonality, rep bias correction.
- **Hybrid:** weighted blend.

**Cadence:** weekly pipeline review, monthly forecast call, quarterly business review.

**Accuracy metrics:** forecast error (MAPE), bias (consistent over/under), coverage ratio (pipeline/quota).

## CRM hygiene and data governance

- **Required fields:** stage, close date, amount, next step, decision criteria, competitor.
- **Stage definitions:** exit criteria per stage (not %).
- **Audit:** weekly hygiene scorecards, automation (validation rules, required fields).
- **Single source of truth:** CRM = system of record for revenue.
