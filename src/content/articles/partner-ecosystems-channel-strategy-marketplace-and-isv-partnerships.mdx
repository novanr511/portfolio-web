No company wins alone. Ecosystems multiply reach.

**Direct answer:** Partner ecosystems extend a company's reach, capabilities, and credibility through structured relationships with channel partners (resellers, distributors, SIs), technology partners (ISVs, OEMs), and marketplaces (cloud, app stores). A successful ecosystem requires clear partner tiers, joint value propositions, aligned incentives, co-marketing, and operational rigor.

## Partner types

**Channel partners (sell/implement):**
- **Resellers/VARs:** buy wholesale, sell retail, add services.
- **Distributors:** fulfill, finance, enable reseller networks.
- **System Integrators (SIs):** implement, customize, manage (GSIs: Accenture, Deloitte; boutique SIs).
- **MSPs/MSSPs:** managed services, recurring revenue.

**Technology partners (integrate/extend):**
- **ISVs (Independent Software Vendors):** build on/extend your platform.
- **OEMs:** embed your tech in their product.
- **Data providers:** enrich your platform.
- **Infrastructure:** cloud, CDN, security, observability.

**Marketplaces:** AWS Marketplace, Azure Marketplace, GCP Marketplace, Salesforce AppExchange, Shopify App Store, Atlassian Marketplace. List → transact → bill.

## Partner program design

**Tier structure:**
- **Registered:** entry, self-serve, basic benefits.
- **Select / Silver:** committed revenue, certified, co-marketing funds (MDF).
- **Premier / Gold / Platinum:** strategic, dedicated PM, joint roadmap, highest discounts, executive sponsor.

**Requirements per tier:** certified staff, revenue targets, certified deals, customer references, training completion.

**Incentives:**
- **Margin/discount:** reseller margin, deal registration protection.
- **MDF (Market Development Funds):** co-marketing reimbursement.
- **Rebates:** retrospective volume/quality bonuses.
- **Deal registration:** protects partner-sourced deals (30–180 days).
- **SPIFFs:** short-term spiffs for strategic motions.

## Channel conflict management

**Rules of engagement:**
- **Deal registration:** first to register owns (30–180 days).
- **Territory/vertical rules:** named accounts direct, rest channel.
- **Direct vs indirect:** rules of engagement, compensation neutrality.
- **Conflict resolution:** escalation path, executive sponsor, arbitration.

## Marketplace strategy

**Listing optimization:** SEO-optimized title/description, screenshots/video, reviews, pricing transparency, free trial.

**Transactable vs non-transactable:** transactable = marketplace handles billing; non-transactable = lead gen.

**Co-sell:** cloud provider sellers co-sell your solution (AWS ACE, Microsoft GTM, Google Cloud Partner Advantage).

**Metrics:** listings views, trials, purchases, revenue, partner-sourced pipeline.

## Partner enablement

**Onboarding:** technical certification, sales certification, sandbox access, demo environments.

**Co-marketing:** joint webinars, case studies, blog swaps, event sponsorship, MDF claims portal.

**Partner portal:** deal registration, deal tracking, MDF claims, training, collateral, deal registration.

**Partner success management:** quarterly business reviews (QBRs), joint pipeline reviews, roadmap alignment.

## Metrics

- **Partner-sourced pipeline / revenue.**
- **Partner-influenced revenue.**
- **Active partners (% of enrolled).**
- **Certification rate.**
- **Deal registration → close rate.**
- **Partner NPS.**
