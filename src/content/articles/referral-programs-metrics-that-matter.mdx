## The metrics

**Qualified conversion.** How many referred signups become customers that fit the target, which the funnel article treats as a stage measure, as the evaluation-rubric article requires for concrete criteria, as the market-definition article advises on defining the target.

**Retention of referred customers.** A referred customer who churns immediately is a cost, which the retention article treats as an economic choice, as the retention-analytics article advises for grouping, as the unit-economics article requires for contribution.

**Fraud-adjusted cost.**

**Incremental lift.** How much of the conversion was caused by the programme, which the geo-experiment guidance tests with a comparison, as the causal-inference article treats as a different question, as the correlation article warns about association.
