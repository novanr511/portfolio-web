Privacy is not a feature. It is a right.

**Direct answer:** Data privacy governs how personal information is collected, processed, stored, shared, and deleted. Major regulations: GDPR (EU), CCPA/CPRA (California), LGPD (Brazil), PDPA (Singapore/Thailand), PIPL (China), APPI (Japan). Privacy by Design embeds privacy into systems from inception. A privacy program covers governance, data mapping, DPIA, rights fulfillment, breach response, and vendor management.

## Key regulations

**GDPR (EU):** lawful basis (consent, contract, legitimate interest), data subject rights (access, rectification, erasure, portability, objection, restriction), DPIA for high-risk processing, DPO appointment, 72-hour breach notification, fines up to 4% global turnover.

**CCPA/CPRA (California):** consumer rights (know, delete, opt-out, correct, limit), business obligations (notice at collection, Do Not Sell/Share, contracts with service providers), enforcement (AG, CPPA), private right of action for breaches.

**Other:** LGPD (Brazil, GDPR-like), PDPA (Singapore/Thailand), PIPL (China, cross-border transfer rules), APPI (Japan), HIPAA (US health), FERPA (education), GLBA (financial).

## Privacy by Design (PbD)

**7 principles (Cavoukian):**
1. Proactive not reactive.
2. Privacy as default.
3. Embedded into design.
4. Full functionality (positive-sum).
5. End-to-end security.
6. Visibility and transparency.
7. Respect for user privacy.

**Implementation:** data minimization, purpose limitation, storage limitation, pseudonymization/anonymization, encryption, access controls, privacy-enhancing technologies (PETs).

## Data governance framework

**Data inventory / catalog:** what data, where, source, purpose, retention, owner, sensitivity.

**Classification:** public, internal, confidential, restricted. Handling rules per class.

**Roles:** Data Controller (determines purpose/means), Data Processor (acts on behalf), DPO (oversight), Data Stewards (operational).

**Policies:** retention schedule, deletion procedures, access control, sharing agreements (DPA/SCC), cross-border transfer mechanisms (SCC, BCR, adequacy).

## Data subject rights fulfillment

**Automation:** self-service portals (access, deletion, portability, rectification, objection).

**Timelines:** GDPR 30 days (extendable 60), CCPA 45 days (extendable 45).

**Verification:** identity verification without excessive data collection.

**Exceptions:** legal obligations, fraud prevention, freedom of expression.

## Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA)

**When required:** systematic profiling, large-scale special category data, public monitoring, new technologies.

**Process:** describe processing, assess necessity/proportionality, identify risks, mitigate, consult DPO, document.

**Review:** at least annually or when processing changes.

## Breach response

**Detection:** monitoring, reporting channels.

**Assessment:** severity, scope, data types, affected individuals.

**Notification:** supervisory authority (72h GDPR), affected individuals (high risk), regulators (sector-specific).

**Containment & remediation:** containment, eradication, recovery, post-incident review.

## Privacy program maturity

**Level 1 (Ad hoc):** reactive, minimal compliance.
**Level 2 (Defined):** policies, DPIA process, basic training.
**Level 3 (Managed):** automated rights fulfillment, DPIA integrated, metrics.
**Level 4 (Optimized):** privacy by default, PETs deployed, continuous improvement, board-level reporting.
