Attribution answers: which marketing dollar worked?

**Direct answer:** Marketing attribution assigns credit for conversions across customer touchpoints. Multi-Touch Attribution (MTA) uses user-level data for granular credit assignment. Marketing Mix Modeling (MMM) uses aggregate time-series data for privacy-safe, channel-level insights. Incrementality testing (geo-lift, holdout) measures true causal impact. Combining methods yields robust budget allocation.

## Attribution approaches

**Single-touch:** first-touch (awareness), last-touch (conversion). Simple, biased.

**Multi-Touch Attribution (MTA):**
- **Rule-based:** linear, time-decay, U-shaped (position-based), W-shaped.
- **Algorithmic / Data-driven:** Shapley value, Markov chains, ML models. Requires user-level data (cookies, IDFA, UTM).

**Marketing Mix Modeling (MMM):** regression on aggregate time-series (spend, impressions, seasonality, macro). No user-level data needed. Privacy-safe. Channel-level granularity.

**Incrementality testing:** controlled experiments (geo-lift, audience holdout, PSA). Measures true causal lift. Gold standard but slow/expensive.

## Choosing the right method

| Scenario | Best fit |
|---|---|
| Digital-first, rich user data | MTA (algorithmic) |
| Omnichannel, offline-heavy | MMM |
| High-stakes budget decisions | Incrementality testing |
| Privacy-first / post-cookie | MMM + incrementality |
| Real-time optimization | MTA + holdout validation |

## Multi-touch models

**Linear:** equal credit to all touches.

**Time-decay:** recency-weighted. Recent touches get more credit.

**U-shaped (Position-based):** 40% first, 40% last, 20% middle.

**W-shaped:** 30% first, 30% lead creation, 30% opportunity creation, 10% other.

**Algorithmic:** Shapley value (game theory), Markov chain removal effect, gradient boosting. Learns from conversion paths.

## Incrementality testing

**Geo-lift:** split DMAs/regions into test/control. Measure lift in test geo.

**Audience holdout:** random user holdout (PSA/ghost ads). Measure conversion difference.

**Synthetic control:** construct synthetic control from donor pool (CausalImpact).

**Design:** power calculation, minimum detectable effect, duration, seasonality control.

## Budget allocation

**Marginal ROI:** allocate next dollar to channel with highest marginal ROAS.

**Diminishing returns:** saturation curves (log, Michaelis-Menten, Hill). Optimize across channels.

**Constraints:** minimum/maximum spend, brand minimums, contractual commitments.

**Scenario planning:** Monte Carlo simulation of budget scenarios.

**Unified framework:** MMM for strategic allocation → MTA for tactical → Incrementality for validation.

## Challenges

- **Walled gardens:** limited data access (Meta, Google, Amazon).
- **Privacy:** cookie deprecation, ATT, GDPR, CCPA. Shift to MMM.
- **Cross-device:** identity resolution incomplete.
- **View-through attribution:** overstates display/video impact.
- **Offline conversions:** store visits, call tracking, CRM match.
