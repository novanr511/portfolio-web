Social media is not broadcasting. It is conversation at scale.

**Direct answer:** Social media marketing uses platforms (Meta, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, X, YouTube, Snapchat, Pinterest) to build brand awareness, engage communities, drive traffic, and generate conversions. Success requires platform-native content, community management, paid amplification, and measurement tied to business outcomes. Algorithms reward engagement, retention, and relevance.

## Platform dynamics (2024 landscape)

**Meta (Facebook/Instagram):** mature, broad demographics. Feed + Reels + Stories. Strong ad targeting. Commerce integration (Shops).

**TikTok:** short-form video, algorithmic discovery. Gen Z/young Millennials. Creator economy native. TikTok Shop.

**YouTube:** long-form + Shorts. Search intent + discovery. High watch time = monetization. Second-largest search engine.

**LinkedIn:** B2B, professional. Thought leadership, employee advocacy, lead gen. Higher CPM, higher intent.

**X (Twitter):** real-time, news, discourse. Brand voice, customer service, thought leadership. Lower reach, high engagement per follower.

**Pinterest:** visual discovery, planning intent. High purchase intent. Long content half-life.

**Snapchat:** AR, young demographics, ephemeral. Geofilters, lenses.

## Content strategy framework

**Pillars:** educate, entertain, inspire, promote (80/20 rule).

**Formats:** short video (Reels/TikTok/Shorts) > carousel > single image > text. Video dominates reach.

**Content calendar:** evergreen + trending + promotional + UGC. Consistency > virality.

**Repurposing:** long-form → clips → carousel → tweet thread → newsletter. One asset, 10+ distributions.

## Community management

**Engagement:** reply to comments, DMs, mentions within SLA. Turn complaints into advocacy.

**User-generated content (UGC):** incentivize, curate, amplify. Social proof > branded content.

**Community building:** groups, Discord, newsletters, events. Move rented audience (followers) to owned (email, CRM).

**Crisis management:** escalation matrix, pre-approved responses, transparency.

## Paid social

**Campaign objectives:** awareness, traffic, engagement, leads, sales (conversions).

**Targeting:** demographic, interest, behavioral, lookalike, custom audiences (pixel, CRM, engagement).

**Creative testing:** hook (0-3s), value prop, social proof, CTA. Iterate fast, kill losers.

**Budget allocation:** 70% proven, 20% iteration, 10% experimentation.

**Measurement:** ROAS, CAC, LTV:CAC, attribution (MTA, incrementality testing).

## Measurement and KPIs

**Awareness:** reach, impressions, share of voice, branded search lift.

**Engagement:** likes, comments, shares, saves, video completion rate.

**Conversion:** clicks, leads, sales, ROAS, CAC, LTV.

**Community:** follower growth, sentiment, UGC volume, community health score.

**Attribution:** last-click underestimates social. Use MTA, MMM, geo-lift, conversion lift studies.
