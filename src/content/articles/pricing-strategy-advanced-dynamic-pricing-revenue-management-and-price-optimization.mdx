Price is the lever with the highest leverage on profit.

**Direct answer:** Advanced pricing strategy goes beyond cost-plus or competitor-based pricing. It uses data science, elasticity modeling, and real-time optimization to maximize revenue or profit. Core disciplines: price elasticity estimation, dynamic pricing algorithms, revenue management (yield management), price discrimination, and psychological pricing. The goal: capture maximum willingness-to-pay across segments and time.

## Price elasticity fundamentals

**Price elasticity of demand (PED):** %ΔQ / %ΔP.
- **Elastic (|PED| > 1):** price cut → revenue up.
- **Inelastic (|PED| < 1):** price hike → revenue up.
- **Unit elastic (|PED| = 1):** revenue unchanged.

**Cross-price elasticity:** substitute (positive) vs complement (negative).

**Income elasticity:** normal (positive) vs inferior (negative).

**Estimation:** regression on historical price/quantity, experimental (A/B tests), conjoint analysis, choice modeling.

## Dynamic pricing

Dynamic pricing adjusts prices in real-time based on demand, inventory, competition, and context.

**Approaches:**
- **Rule-based:** if-then rules (time, inventory, competitor price).
- **Algorithmic:** ML models predict demand at price points, optimize for revenue/profit.
- **Reinforcement learning:** agent learns pricing policy via simulation.

**Applications:**
- **Airlines/hotels:** yield management (booking curves, overbooking).
- **Ride-sharing:** surge pricing (Uber, Lyft).
- **E-commerce:** Amazon-style repricing (competitor tracking).
- **Events/sports:** dynamic ticket pricing.
- **Cloud/utility:** spot instances, demand response.

**Guardrails:** price floors/ceilings, brand constraints, fairness rules, regulatory compliance.

## Revenue management (yield management)

RM maximizes revenue from fixed, perishable capacity.

**Core levers:**
- **Capacity allocation:** segment allocation (booking classes).
- **Overbooking:** statistical no-show models.
- **Duration control:** minimum stay, length-of-stay restrictions.
- **Pricing:** dynamic rates by channel, lead time, segment.

**KPIs:** RevPAR (Rev per Available Room), RevPASH (Rev per Available Seat Hour), GOPPAR, load factor, yield.

## Price discrimination (versioning)

Capturing consumer surplus by offering versions at different prices.

**Types:**
- **First-degree (perfect):** each customer pays max WTP. Theoretical.
- **Second-degree (self-selection):** quantity discounts, versioning (Basic/Pro/Enterprise), bundling.
- **Third-degree (segment-based):** student/senior discounts, geographic pricing, channel pricing.

**Menu design:** good-better-best, anchor pricing, decoy effect.

## Price optimization techniques

- **Elasticity modeling:** log-log regression, Bayesian hierarchical models, random forests for heterogeneous elasticity.
- **A/B testing:** price experiments with proper randomization, sufficient power.
- **Conjoint analysis:** discrete choice experiments for WTP estimation.
- **Van Westendorp / Gabor-Granger:** survey-based price sensitivity.
- **Reinforcement learning:** contextual bandits for real-time pricing.

## Psychological pricing

- **Charm pricing:** $9.99 vs $10.00 (left-digit effect).
- **Prestige pricing:** round numbers for luxury ($1000 vs $999).
- **Anchoring:** show higher reference price first.
- **Decoy effect:** add dominated option to shift preference.
- **Bundle pricing:** perceived value > sum of parts.

## Implementation roadmap

1. **Audit current pricing:** price waterfall, discount leakage, elasticity gaps.
2. **Build elasticity models:** historical + experimental data.
3. **Pilot:** single product/segment, A/B test.
4. **Scale:** dynamic pricing engine, guardrails, monitoring.
4. **Governance:** pricing council, approval workflows, competitive intelligence.
