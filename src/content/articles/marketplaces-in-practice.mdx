**Cold start.** A marketplace with no sellers has nothing for buyers, and vice versa, which the cold-start article treats as a problem with strategies and costs. **Liquidity.** The relevant measure is not total users but whether a participant can find a match quickly, which the network-effects article treats as the test of value, as the funnel article advises for stage measures. <Mark>A large marketplace with slow matching is weaker than a small one that clears quickly</Mark>, as the market-definition article warns about defining the right market.
