## The rules

**Normalise the host and scheme.** One canonical host and one scheme avoid duplicate versions, which the URL-structure article advises, as the canonical article explains for signals, as the HTTPS article advises on certificate and redirect diagnosis.

**Keep paths readable and stable.** <Mark>A path that describes the page survives better than an opaque identifier</Mark>, which the URL-structure article advises, as the internal-linking article treats links as navigation, as the information-architecture article argues for grouping.

**Control parameters.** Tracking and filter parameters can create many versions of one page, which the URL-structure article advises normalising, as the faceted-navigation article warns about parameter variation, as the canonical article treats duplicate URLs as a signal problem.

**Plan changes as migrations.** A URL change needs redirects and a check, which the site-migration checklist requires, as the redirect article advises on chains, as the server-logs article recommends for verifying what was requested.
